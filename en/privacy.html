Privacy Policy

PocketToDo (the "App") is designed with your privacy at its core. This policy explains what data the App handles, and how.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

Your content — tasks, settings, themes, and custom palettes — is stored only on your device (on-device) and is never sent to the developer or any third-party server. No account or sign-in is required. (Purchase processing is the only exception — see "In-App Purchases" below.)

Sharing with Third Parties

We do not share, sell, lease, or transfer your task and settings content to third parties. The only exception is purchase processing: as described under "In-App Purchases" below, Apple and RevenueCat handle purchase data (anonymously, without identifying you).

Analytics, Tracking, and Advertising

The App does not embed any analytics or behavioral-measurement SDKs such as Google Analytics, Firebase, or Adjust. No advertising SDKs are present. The App does not reference IDFA or IDFV.

In-App Purchases (Pro Features)

Payment for Pro subscriptions and the one-time purchase is handled by Apple's App Store and StoreKit. Card and payment details are managed by Apple; the developer has no access to them.

Purchase state management and receipt validation are handled by a third-party service, RevenueCat. RevenueCat processes your purchase and subscription history linked to an anonymous identifier (no name, email, or other personal information is sent). This enables purchase restoration and anonymous revenue aggregation. See the privacy policies of Apple and RevenueCat for details.

Use by Minors

Because the App does not collect any personal information, it can be used safely regardless of age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as features are added. Material changes will be announced on this page. The "Last updated" date below reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

For any questions about this policy, please reach out via our Support page.
