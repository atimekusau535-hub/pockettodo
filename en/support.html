Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

Your tasks and settings live on your device and are never sent to the developer. The only exception is purchase processing, where Apple and RevenueCat handle purchase data anonymously. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Does data carry over when I change my iPhone?

Your data is stored on your device. For now, there's no automatic transfer between devices (you can export a CSV to keep a record).

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

The free plan includes capture, scene switching, scene-mapping customization, manual export, and the default + dark themes. Pro unlocks: full-history (no 7-day cutoff), additional color themes plus your own custom palettes, and renaming the 4 weights (names and meanings) in your own words.

How do I cancel Pro?

Open the iPhone Settings app → [your name] → Subscriptions → PocketToDo. Your task data will remain even after canceling.

The app icon changed when I switched themes

That's by design. The app icon follows the selected theme. Switch back to "sumi (default)" to restore the original icon.

Are there notifications?

By design, no. PocketToDo is built to not nag. We aim for natural recall — capturing as you remember, and surfacing as you switch scenes.

I found a bug

Please reach out on X (@bon_dev_). If you can include device model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce, it helps us fix it faster.
